Your cooperative has signed up to a Living Income project in cocoa, in which the incomes and expenditures of cocoa farmer households are measured and analysed, in order to gain insights into the effectiveness of Fairtrade's Living Income Strategy. The records will be used to monitor how household incomes improve over time and help your cooperative identify appropriate support services to bridge the income gap.

This tool captures the data you have entered in your farm record book, and is intended to facilitate the analysis of aggregated data of all participating cooperatives and their members. The farm record data are captured by trained coaches from your cooperative. Your data can be shared with Fairtrade International, Fairtrade Africa and your buyers, based on your consent.

Fairtrade International will treat all information you provide as confidential. It will be stored securely and not shared without your permission. Findings from across all participating organizations will only be communicated externally in a combined and anonymous manner.

Participation in this project is entirely voluntary. If you agree to participate, you can still decide not to share specific data, and this will not affect you or your cooperative in any way. However, we encourage you to share your complete records to allow for realistic measurement of the results of this project and help Fairtrade improve its strategy design.
